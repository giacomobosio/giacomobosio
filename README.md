## Giacomo Bosio

Certified **Web Accessibility Expert (UNI 11621-3)**, CEO and co-founder of [Hedron](https://hedron.it/), Adjunct Professor at Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore in Milan.

I help companies and public bodies meet their accessibility obligations (European Accessibility Act, EN 301 549, WCAG 2.2) and build digital products that people can actually use.

### What I work on

- Accessibility audits and accessibility statements (EN 301 549 / WCAG 2.2)
- Accessible design systems and product design
- Training for developers and content teams
- UX research and usability testing with real users

### Open source
